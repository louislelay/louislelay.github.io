Louis Le Lay
locomotion & simulation @ uma

I'm Louis Le Lay. I build learning-based controllers to make humanoid robots agile and useful.

At UMA, I lead locomotion and simulation for ASTRO, from bare hardware to walking and whole-body teleoperation. I deploy the same stack across the Unitree G1, AgiBot X2, and Booster K1.

Previously, I built the first RL locomotion controller for PAL Robotics' Kangaroo, a serial-parallel humanoid, replacing its legacy MPC walker. I am also a co-author of mjlab and an active contributor to Isaac Lab.
